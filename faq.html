FAQs

Do I pay on the website?

No. Nothing is charged when you send a form. We first confirm everything with you in our Instagram chat, and you pay only after you say yes.

Can I change something after I send it?

Yes. Just tell us in our Instagram chat. You can change details any time before we start making your piece.

I filled the form. What now?

Tap "Copy & open Instagram", paste the message in our chat and tap Send. We'll reply there with the next steps.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in India, with tracking.

How long does it take?

Making takes a minimum of 5 working days, and shipping within India takes a further 3 working days.
