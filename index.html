Our story

A mother, two daughters and a lot of clay

The Subtle Side is a homegrown brand that makes clay miniatures and handicrafts. We started during the 2020 lockdown, and what began at the kitchen table has grown into a range of products that bring art and memories to life.

From the clay to the final piece, everything is handmade. Our products are made from air-dry clay that we make ourselves, and every piece is designed to carry the emotions of the giver into something beautiful for the receiver.
